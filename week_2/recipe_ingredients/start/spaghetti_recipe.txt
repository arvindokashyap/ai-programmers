Spaghetti with Meat Sauce

1 lb spaghetti
1 Tbl olive oil
1 lb ground beef
1 onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp salt
1/2 tsp pepper
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp dried basil
1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
28 oz can crushed tomatoes
6 oz tomato paste
1/2 C water
1 tsp sugar

To serve:
1/2 C grated parmesan
2 Tbl fresh basil, chopped

Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onion and cook about 5 minutes until soft. Add garlic, cook 1 minute more.
Add ground beef, break it up and cook until browned, drain the fat.
Stir in salt, pepper, oregano, basil and red pepper flakes. Add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, water and sugar. Bring to a simmer, then turn down to low and let it cook 20-30 minutes, stirring now and then.

Meanwhile boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente, drain.

Serve sauce over spaghetti, top with parmesan and fresh basil.
